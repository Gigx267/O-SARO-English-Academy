Answers

Your questions, answered

We’ve grouped the most common questions below. If something’s still unclear, reach out — we’re happy to talk it through.

Do I need to take a placement test first?

Yes — we start with a free assessment call and a short level task. It takes about 30 minutes and tells us exactly which programme fits you best. There’s no charge and no obligation to enroll afterwards.

How large are the groups?

Strictly 8 to 10 students maximum. We cap every cohort so that each person gets speaking time in every session and written feedback on every piece of work. No exceptions.

Are classes online or in person?

All classes run live online, so you can join from anywhere in the world. Sessions are interactive and camera-on by default — this is a conversation, not a webinar. You’ll need a stable internet connection, a microphone, and a webcam.

How long does a course take?

Most exam tracks run for 10 to 12 weeks with two sessions per week, plus homework. General B2 is more flexible and can be extended term by term. We’ll map out a realistic timeline during your assessment call.

What if I don’t reach my target score?

You can rejoin a later cohort at a reduced rate. Our 90% first-attempt pass rate means it rarely comes to that — but we don’t leave anyone stranded. We’ll also give you a clear go/no-go recommendation before you book the real exam.

Which exam should I choose — Cambridge B2 First, IELTS, or TOEFL?

It depends on your goal. Cambridge B2 First is ideal for general certification and European universities. IELTS is usually required for UK, Australian, and Canadian immigration or university admission. TOEFL iBT is most common for US universities. We’ll help you decide during your assessment call.

Do you offer General English or levels below B2?

We specialise exclusively in B2 (Upper-Intermediate) and exam preparation at that level. If you’re not quite there yet, we can recommend trusted partners for lower-level courses.

What happens if I miss a class?

All sessions are recorded and shared with the group (with participants’ consent). You’ll also receive the materials and homework. If you miss more than two sessions in a row, we’ll schedule a short catch-up call to keep you on track.

How much does a course cost?

Pricing depends on the programme and duration. We keep our fees competitive for small-group specialised instruction. Contact us for a detailed quote — we’ll also let you know about any early-bird or bundle discounts.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, we offer a two-payment plan for most courses. The first payment secures your seat, and the second is due halfway through the course. Get in touch to discuss options.

Is there a refund policy?

If you cancel more than 7 days before the start date, you’ll receive a full refund minus a small administrative fee. After the course begins, refunds are pro-rated only in exceptional circumstances. Full details are provided upon enrollment.

Who are the instructors?

All our instructors are CELTA/DELTA-qualified native or near-native speakers with extensive experience preparing students for Cambridge, IELTS, and TOEFL exams. The lead instructor personally oversees every cohort.

How much homework is there?

Expect around 3–5 hours per week, including writing tasks, reading, and listening practice. We keep it focused and purposeful — no busywork. Every written task receives individual feedback.
