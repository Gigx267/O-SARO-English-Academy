Our story

Founded on a simple frustration.

Too many students were passing exams without ever feeling comfortable in English — and too many classes were too large to notice.

🌱

Why we started

O’saro English Academy began with a decision to do one thing properly instead of everything adequately. We teach B2 — the level where learners stop translating in their head and start thinking in English — and we teach it in rooms small enough that every voice is heard in every session.

That means no lecture halls, no pre-recorded replacements, and no cohort so large that your writing comes back with a tick and a grade instead of a comment. Every essay is read. Every question is answered. Every student speaks, weekly.

Today we work with learners worldwide, live and online, preparing for Cambridge B2 First, IELTS Academic & General, TOEFL iBT, or simply for a stronger, calmer command of the language.

B2 specialists Live classes only Worldwide

Quiet focus, not noise

Our sessions are calm by design. Fewer students, longer speaking turns, and space to think before you answer — because that is how fluency actually forms.

What we believe

Four principles we don’t compromise on.

These aren’t slogans — they decide how we build every cohort, every week.

🫧

Calm over cramming

Steady, weekly progress beats last-minute panic. We build routines you can keep.

📐

Depth over tricks

Exam technique matters — but real comprehension is what carries you through.

✍️

Feedback over flattery

Honest, specific corrections on every piece of writing — returned before the next class.

👥

Small over scalable

We cap every group at ten. Growth is not worth losing the room.
