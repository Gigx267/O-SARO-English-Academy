Our story

It started with one student and one stubborn problem.

O’saro began in 2019 as a single evening tutoring slot. One learner, stuck at intermediate for years, convinced us that the problem was never ability — it was the format.

📖

From cramming to comprehension

Large classes reward the loudest voice and the fastest memoriser. We wanted the opposite: a room small enough that every hesitation gets noticed, and slow enough that grammar actually settles into speech. That first student passed B2 First in eleven weeks — and then sent three friends.

🧘

Small, on purpose

We have deliberately never grown past a handful of cohorts per term. Every group is capped at ten, every essay is marked by the person who taught the lesson, and every learner gets speaking time in every single session. Growth has never been the goal — retention has.

Our approach

How a term at O’saro actually runs

A predictable four-step rhythm, repeated every term, so you always know what comes next.

01

Assess

A free 30-minute call plus a short written task. We map your true level across all four skills before you commit to anything.

02

Place

You’re matched to a cohort of similar level and goal — Cambridge, IELTS, TOEFL or general B2 — never a mixed-ability room.

03

Practise

Two live sessions a week, camera-on, with authentic past papers, timed tasks and weekly written corrections on every essay.

04

Certify

Full mock exams under real conditions, then guided registration. And if you miss your target, you rejoin a later cohort at a reduced rate.

Questions

About the academy

Still unsure about something? Call us and we’ll talk it through.

Who is O’saro actually for?

Learners at B1 aiming for B2, and B2 learners polishing towards C1. If you’re a complete beginner, we’re not the right fit — and we’ll tell you that honestly on the first call.

Do I need to take a placement test first?

Yes — we start with a free assessment call and a short level task. It takes about 30 minutes and tells us exactly which programme fits you best.

Why do you cap groups at ten?

Because past ten, someone stops speaking. The cap is the single most important design decision we’ve made, and we won’t raise it to grow the school.

Are classes online or in person?

All classes run live online, so you can join from anywhere. Sessions are interactive and camera-on by default — this is a conversation, not a webinar.

What if I don’t reach my target score?

You can rejoin a later cohort at a reduced rate. Our 90% first-attempt pass rate means it rarely comes to that — but we don’t leave anyone stranded.
